DebugAssist

An AI assistant for investigating and fixing software problems

When software breaks, engineers often spend a lot of time figuring out what went wrong before they can fix it. DebugAssist helps with that process. It investigates errors, looks for the likely cause, suggests a code change, and checks the result. What began as a proof of concept during my internship is now a production product used by more than 1,500 engineers at Uber.
